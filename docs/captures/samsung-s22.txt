--- How to read this ---
    A small file gives a low rate. The cost of one operation does
    not change with the size, so the cost counts for more.

    A larger read size gives a higher rate, up to a limit. The
    limit is the link, or the device.

    A rate far under the estimate, with a large file and a large
    read size, points at the hub chain or the cable.
